NativaCare’s nanny training is delivered personally by a registered midwife, in homes across Abu Dhabi and Dubai. It isn’t a generic workshop or a video course. It’s structured, hands-on, and built around your baby, your home, and your routine.

FAQ's

Is professional midwifery care available at home in Abu Dhabi?

Yes. NativaCare is a registered midwife who comes directly to the family’s home across Abu Dhabi’s main communities, including Khalidiyah, Al Reem Island, Saadiyat Island, Yas Island, Al Mushrif, Al Nahyan, Al Bateen, and the Corniche area.

How is midwifery care different from seeing a doctor or gynaecologist?

A gynaecologist handles the clinical and medical side of pregnancy. A midwife focuses on education, preparation, emotional support, feeding, and recovery. Most families benefit from both, NativaCare fills the gap that short hospital appointments don’t have time for.

What should I look for in a maternity care provider in Abu Dhabi?

Credentials you can verify, continuity of care (the same person every visit), and a provider licensed to practise in the emirate. NativaCare offers all three.

How do I know a midwifery service in Abu Dhabi is legitimate?

Check that the midwife holds a current Department of Health licence to practise in Abu Dhabi alongside recognised professional qualifications. Always ask about credentials before booking, NativaCare’s are open to check.

Do families need to travel for midwifery support?

No. Every service is delivered at home across Abu Dhabi. There are no clinic visits required when the midwife comes to you.

Which areas of Abu Dhabi does NativaCare cover?

Khalidiyah, Al Reem Island, Saadiyat Island, Yas Island, Al Mushrif, Al Nahyan, Al Bateen, the Corniche area, and surrounding communities. Get in touch to confirm availability in your neighbourhood.
